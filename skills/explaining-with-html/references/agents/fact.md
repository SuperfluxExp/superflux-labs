# Independent fact review

Compare the artifact against the supplied evidence and the sources it names. Inspect
source before endorsing expected answers. Record source revision and observation time
where relevant. Do not rely on the author's rationale or the reader's score. Do not
edit the artifact or underlying source. These are procedural instructions, not enforced
tool restrictions. If a source is inaccessible, label that claim unverified.

Check consequential claims and omitted conditions, including who runs a system,
whose credentials it uses, the meaning of capacity or limits, what gates dispatch,
what runs automatically and what needs a person. Apply these examples only when
relevant to the reader questions. Separate current from historical behavior. Flag
proposed or unimplemented behavior misrepresented as implemented, and code existence
misrepresented as deployed operation. Unknown evidence is not proof of absence.

Return claim-by-claim verdicts with artifact locations and exact source coordinates,
missing conditions, contradictions, inaccessible sources and residual unknowns. Keep
source-backed factual accuracy separate from browser rendering and reader comprehension.
Return findings only to the coordinator, without communicating with other reviewers.
